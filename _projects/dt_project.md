## Design Process

The project followed a systematic design methodology including user research, specification development, iterative design, and testing. Six initial concepts were developed and evaluated against client requirements before selecting the final goblet-shaped wall-mounted design.

### Design Iterations

Six design concepts were evaluated against specifications including barrel-shaped storage, rotating platforms, and wall-mounted configurations.

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Capacity**: 18 standard wine bottles + 2 goblets + 8 bottle stoppers
- **Dimensions**: 640mm (W) × 1150mm (H) × 240mm (D)
- **Material**: 5mm plywood (laser-cut layers) with varnish finish
- **Weight**: Medium (requires two-person installation)
- **Mounting**: Wall-hung system with chain supports and brackets

**Key CAD Skills:**
- 3D modeling in SketchUp for concept visualization
- Orthographic projection generation for manufacturing
- Exploded assembly views for construction planning
- 2D Design software for laser cutting file preparation
- Material rendering and texture application

### Manufacturing Integration

The design integrated multiple fabrication methods:
- **Laser cutting**: Precision cutting of plywood layers with optimized settings (87% power, 5% speed)
- **Material testing**: Systematic evaluation of plywood, jelutong, and melamine for laser compatibility
- **Hybrid fabrication**: Combined laser cutting for complex curves with bandsaw work for structural elements
- **Assembly**: Nail and screw joinery reinforced with PVA adhesive

## Outcomes

**Strengths:**
- Unique aesthetic with vintage wooden appearance meeting client preferences
- Functional storage meeting capacity requirements (20 bottles)
- Multi-purpose design accommodating bottles, goblets, and accessories
- Professional finish with glossy waterproof varnish

**Development Opportunities:**
- Floor-standing variant for improved stability
- Transparent door addition for UV protection while maintaining visibility
- Horizontal bottle orientation for better accessibility
- Material optimization for sustainability (single timber pieces vs. laminated plywood)

### Client Validation

Prototype testing with target users confirmed ergonomic accessibility and aesthetic appeal while identifying refinements for production versions, particularly regarding safety features and bottle retrieval mechanisms.
